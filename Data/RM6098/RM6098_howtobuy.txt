How to buy Technology Products & Associated Services 2 (RM6098):

There are 3 ways to buy from this agreement:

option 1: run a further competition
option 2: direct award (buy directly from a single supplier)
option 3: online through our Purchasing Platform catalogue (buy low value and low complexity items)
If this is your first time using Purchasing Platform, you will need to register for an account. If you already have an account you can sign in to the platform.

The buyer guide, in the documents section below, explains the different buying options in more detail.

If you would like to use the CCS eSourcing tool as your procurement tool register or sign in. For more information, read the CCS eSourcing tool guidance.

Aggregation
We have a complimentary aggregation service to bring together customers with similar requirements to maximise savings.

CCS manages the aggregation procurement process from start to finish. Email aggregation@crowncommercial.gov.uk for further details.
