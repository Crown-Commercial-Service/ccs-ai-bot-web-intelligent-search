Framework Name: Public Sector Legal Services  
Framework Number: RM6240  
Category: Professional Services  

Objective:  
The Public Sector Legal Services framework provides a streamlined and compliant route to market for obtaining a comprehensive range of legal services. It is designed specifically for public sector organizations, covering widely needed legal areas such as contracts, dispute resolution, and corporate law. The framework comprises three lots, offering both general and specialized legal services.  

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors: Wider public sector  
- Service Types:  
  - Employment Law  
  - Pensions  
  - Education Law  
  - Health and Safety  
  - Licensing Law  
  - Immigration  
  - Property and Construction  
  - Litigation/Dispute Resolution  
  - Social Housing  

Restrictions (if any):  
None specified
