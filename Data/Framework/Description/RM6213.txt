**Framework Name**: Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Solutions (VCIS)  
**Framework Number**: RM6213  
**Category**: Digital and Technology Services  

**Objective**:  
The Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Solutions (VCIS) framework provides central government and public sector entities with access to a range of products and services to support the development of electric and hybrid vehicle charging infrastructure. It allows buyers to procure either full end-to-end solutions or specific elements as needed to facilitate the transition to low emission vehicles.

**Scope**:
- Eligible Sectors: Central government, wider public sector
- Service Types:
  - Consultancy and feasibility studies
  - Civil design and installation
  - Provision, installation, and maintenance of charging hardware
  - Software and back-office solutions
  - Lease and purchase options for products
  - Comprehensive end-to-end service solutions

**Restrictions**:  
- No maximum contract length
- Unlimited supplier spaces, contingent on meeting selection criteria
- No cost for suppliers to join the agreement

**Additional Information**:  
CCS supports the deployment of EV infrastructure via two agreements: Transport Technology and Associated Services (TTAS) and VCIS. A Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS) is used for filtering relevant suppliers. Explore key differences and procurement scenarios on the provided [VCIS agreement page](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6213).
