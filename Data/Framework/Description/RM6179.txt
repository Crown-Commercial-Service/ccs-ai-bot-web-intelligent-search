Framework Name: Legal Services Panel
Framework Number: RM6179
Category: Professional Services
Objective: The Legal Services Panel agreement offers general and complex legal advice and services to central government. It aims to streamline the procurement process for a wide range of commonly needed legal areas, including contracts, dispute resolution, real estate, and corporate finance.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: Central government departments and executive agencies
- Service Types:
  - General legal advice and services (Lot 1)
  - Finance and complex legal services (Lot 2)
  - Specialized legal areas including contracts, dispute resolution, corporate finance, employment law, high-value M&A, intellectual property, public procurement, real estate, rescue and insolvency, innovative and complex projects, and public inquiries
  
Restrictions: 
- Suppliers provide services primarily under English and Welsh law, but may also offer advice on Scots, Northern Irish, and international law.
- Call-off contracts must expire no later than 2 years after the agreement end date.
