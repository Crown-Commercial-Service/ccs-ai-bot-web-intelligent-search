Framework Name: Security - Physical, Technical and Support Services  
Framework Number: RM6257  
Category: Facilities Management  

Objective: This agreement provides access to a wide range of security products and services for all UK public sector bodies. It aims to ensure comprehensive security solutions including guarding, installation of systems, and consultancy services across various regions.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: All UK public sector bodies
- Service types: 
  * Guarding services (static/mobile patrolling, reception manning)
  * Visitor management and video surveillance monitoring
  * Installation/maintenance of security systems (alarms, fencing, locks)
  * Consultancy services
  * Risk and security assessments

Restrictions:
- Provides services across 40 UK regions defined by the Nomenclature of Territorial Statistics (NUTS), with potential international opportunities.
- Consultants must sign a non-disclosure agreement and have their organisation complete a consultant approval form.
- Necessary documents must be emailed for validation.
