Framework Name: Automation Marketplace DPS    
Framework Number: RM6173    
Category: Software    

Objective: This agreement provides intelligent automation services and solutions, including technologies, consultancy, services, and licences, aimed at streamlining processes and systems for public sector customers.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: Public sector, associated bodies, and agencies
- Service Types:
  - Strategy and business transformation
  - Problem solving with tech solutions
  - Resources and training
  - Software licences

Restrictions:
- Out of scope: 
  - Hardware and infrastructure
  - Hosting
  - Networks or connectivity services
