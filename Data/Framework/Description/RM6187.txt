Framework Name: Management Consultancy Framework Three (MCF3)

Framework Number: RM6187

Category: Professional Services

Objective: Central government and wider public sector customers can access cost-effective consultancy advice from a range of suppliers across nine specific lots. This framework aims to provide specialized management consultancy services to enhance efficiencies in business operations and governance.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: Central government, wider public sector
- Service Types: Business, strategy and policy, finance, HR, procurement, health/social care and community, infrastructure and environment

Restrictions:
- Duration: 4 years (no option to extend)
- Replacement: RM6309 MCF4 is currently being procured as the replacement agreement. More information can be found on the RM6309 MCF4 webpage.
