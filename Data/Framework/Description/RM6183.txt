**Framework Name:** Trade Law Panel  
**Framework Number:** RM6183  
**Category:** Professional Services  

**Objective:**  
Provide specialist legal advice and support for international trade law, disputes, and negotiations. This agreement assists central government departments and devolved administrations in accessing worldwide legal expertise through a simple route to market, created in partnership with the Department for International Trade.  

**Scope:**  
- Eligible sectors: Central government departments, devolved administrations  
- Service types:  
  - Advice on all stages of international trade disputes  
  - Compliance with World Trade Organisation (WTO) law  
  - Legal advice on the implementation of trade agreements  

**Restrictions:**  
- Agreement duration: 4 years, no options to extend  
- Procurement methods: Further competition (including accelerated competitions), direct award  

For more information, visit the [Trade Law Panel agreement page](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6183).
