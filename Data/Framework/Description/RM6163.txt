Framework Name: Workforce Improvement Services  
Framework Number: RM6163  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  

Objective:  
The Workforce Improvement Services agreement enables NHS trusts and other contracting authorities to appoint experienced workforce partners for developing and improving workforce solutions. Suppliers provide tailored solutions to address your specific workforce challenges, ensuring comprehensive and managed service delivery.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: NHS, healthcare sector, accessible to all contracting authorities
- Service types: Permanent recruitment, temporary appointments, management of flexible workforce solutions (e.g., staff banks)

Restrictions:  
This agreement initially lasts for 2 years and can be extended up to a total of 4 years.
