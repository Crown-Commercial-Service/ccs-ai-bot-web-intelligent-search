Framework Name: Construction Professional Services DPS  
Framework Number: RM6242  
Category: Construction  

Objective: The Construction Professional Services DPS provides central government and wider public sector organizations with access to expert professional and technical construction services. This DPS offers a quick, simple, and flexible route to market through an extensive number of suppliers.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors:
  - Central Government
  - Wider Public Sector Organizations  
- Service Types:
  - Project management
  - Cost management
  - Engineering (e.g., civil, structural, electrical)
  - Architectural and design services
  - Environmental and sustainability services  
- Project Types:
  - New builds
  - Refurbishment of buildings and infrastructure
  - Regeneration of urban areas, including upgrading transport links
  - Alteration and extension of buildings
  - Conservation and preservation projects
  - Maintenance works and programs
  - Design and build of security/counter-terrorism defenses
  - Demolitions of buildings and infrastructure

Restrictions: Projects must utilize the DPS filter system to find local suppliers. Suppliers must access the supplier bid pack to provide services through this DPS.
