Framework Name: Mobile Voice and Data Services
Framework Number: RM6261
Category: Network Services
Objective: Provide UK public sector organisations access to comprehensive mobile voice and data services, including optional and professional services. Enhance communication and data management through secure and integrated solutions.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All UK public sector organisations
- Service Types:
  - UK and international airtime including roaming, calls and short message services (SMS)
  - Data plans
  - Device hardware and accessories
  - Supporting services (e.g., mobile device management and security)
  - Audit and health check services, including current spend and usage analysis
  - Professional services (e.g., system and service integration, implementation services, service design consultation)
  - Security solutions, including enhanced end-to-end software encryption

Restrictions:
- Agreement length: 2+1+1 format (initial 2 years with two possible 1-year extensions)
- No restrictions on the duration of call-off contracts, allowing flexibility based on organizational needs.
