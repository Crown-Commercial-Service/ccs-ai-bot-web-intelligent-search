Framework Name: Employee Benefits and Services  
Framework Number: RM6273  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  

Objective:
Facilitate access and provision of a range of employee benefits through a centrally branded online employee benefits platform. Enhance workforce satisfaction and retention within the public sector.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government, arms length bodies, wider public sector
- Service types: Employee benefits provision, online benefits platform

Restrictions:
- Duration: 3 years with an option to extend by 1 year
- Recommended call-off contract length: Not longer than 4 years

For more information, visit: [Employee Benefits and Services - RM6273](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6273)
