Framework Name:
Construction Works and Associated Services 2 (CWAS2) / ProCure 23 (P23)

Framework Number:
RM6267

Category:
Construction

Objective:
Provide a range of design, construction, and building services for NHS England and other UK public sector bodies. Offer flexible contract solutions tailored to various construction needs, including major and minor building works, civil engineering projects, and NHS capital projects. Enhance service procurement through advanced contract arrangements and promote government policies on BIM, lifecycle cost, and early contractor involvement.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: NHS England, UK public sector bodies, Social Care organisations
- Service Types:
  - Design and construction services for NHS capital projects
  - Maintenance and repairs of buildings and facilities
  - Major and minor building works
  - General construction projects
  - Civil engineering projects

Restrictions:
- Lots 1 to 3 apply to capital health projects in England only
- Lots 4 and 5 provide increased capacity for larger value building works and general construction projects, available nationally
