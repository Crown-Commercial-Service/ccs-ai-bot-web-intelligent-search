Framework Name: Estate Management Services  
Framework Number: RM6168  
Category: Facilities Management  

Objective:  
Provides public sector organisations with a simplified means of sourcing end-to-end property management services, from consultancy to disposal. This framework aims to reduce property costs and release unwanted property assets while leveraging innovation and technology advancements.

Scope:  
- Eligible sectors: Public sector organisations  
- Service types:  
  - Total estate management  
  - Estate (property) management  
  - Agency and lease management  
  - Surveying and strategic advice  
  - Valuation and compulsory sale orders  
  - Business rating services and integrated workplace management services  
  - Integrator services  

Restrictions:  
- Agreement duration: 3 years, with an option for a 1-year extension  

For further details, visit: [Estate Management Services RM6168](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6168)
