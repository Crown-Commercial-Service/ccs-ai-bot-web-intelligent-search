Framework Name: Cyber Security Services 3  
Framework Number: RM3764.3   
Category: Digital and Technology Services  
Objective: A flexible commercial agreement offering a comprehensive range of cyber security services aimed at enhancing organisational cyber resilience and security posture.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: UK central government departments, wider public sector organisations, charities
- Service types:
  - NCSC assured services
  - Consultancy and advice
  - Penetration testing
  - Incident response
  - Managed security services

Restrictions: None
