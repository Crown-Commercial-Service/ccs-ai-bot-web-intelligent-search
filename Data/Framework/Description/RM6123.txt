Framework Name: Media Services  
Framework Number: RM6123  
Category: Professional Services  

Objective: This framework allows central government and the wider public sector to access world-class communication services by purchasing advertising space, forming partnerships, utilizing out of home (OOH) advertising, auditing the performance of their agencies, and monetizing their own advertising estates.

Scope: 
- Eligible Sectors:
  - Central government
  - Local government
  - Charities
  - Health organisations
  - Arms-length bodies (ALBs)
- Service Types:
  - Strategic media activation (Media Buying)
  - Public sector media planning and buying
  - Out of home media
  - Advertising revenue generation
  - Service evaluation and performance

Restrictions: 
- Agreement Duration: 4 years, expiring on 13 June 2026
- No options to extend
- Term of call-off contracts cannot extend beyond the expiry date
