Framework Name: Audit & Assurance Services (A&AS)

Framework Number: RM6188

Category: Professional Services

Objective: Provide central government and wider public sector with cost-effective audit and assurance services. These services include internal and external audit, counter fraud, and investigation, ensuring comprehensive oversight and accountability.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: Central government, wider public sector
- Service Types:
  - Internal audit and assurance
  - External audit
  - Counter fraud and investigation
  - Other independent assurance

Restrictions: This agreement will run for 4 years with no option to extend. RM6310 A&AS2 is being procured as the replacement agreement. For more information, visit the A&AS2 webpage. 

More details are available in the lot description document.
