Framework Name: Travel, Transport, Accommodation and Venue Solutions
Framework Number: RM6342
Category: Travel, Accommodation and Venues

Objective: This agreement provides comprehensive travel, accommodation, and venue solutions along with support services for individuals under public sector care, both within the UK and overseas. It aims to facilitate business and emergency travel arrangements and support welfare, security, and translation services for specific groups.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government, wider public sector, and third-sector organisations.
- Service types:
  - Air booking services (including chartered planes and helicopters)
  - Rail booking services
  - Accommodation booking services
  - Ground transportation (including vehicle hire)
  - Ferry booking services
  - Commercial accommodation (hotels, apartments, holiday parks, halls of residence, vessels)
  - Venue hire for events
  - Ancillary services (visas, passports, currency)
  - Travel crisis response services (evacuation flights, chartered flights, crisis accommodation, COVID-19 test kits, security)

Restrictions:
- Not explicitly stated; refer to the buyer guide for authorized customers and specific details.
