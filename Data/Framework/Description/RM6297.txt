Framework Name: Print and Digital Communications  
Framework Number: RM6297  
Category: Hardware  

Objective:
The Print and Digital Communications agreement provides comprehensive managed print products and services, including bulk printing, direct mail, and secure print. It is designed for public sector organizations requiring the expertise of a print manager. Customers can access services through two distinct lots based on their purchasing needs.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All public sector organizations across the UK
- Service Types:
  - Lot 1: Single supplier (direct award for command and house paper services)
  - Lot 2: Multiple suppliers (further competition)
  - Services available:
    - Command and house paper services (Lot 1 only)
    - Bulk printing and full range of print products (envelopes, labels, folders)
    - Operational print (brochures, leaflets, business cards, business stationery)
    - Finishing services (binding, stamping, embossing, laminating)
    - Alternative formats (easy read, large print, alternative languages, audio, braille)
    - Secure print services and items
    - Print stock management and distribution
    - Digital solutions and transformation services (email, SMS, website)
    - Election print and mailing services
  - Associated services: 
    - Scanning and microfiche services
    - Secure shredding of confidential waste 
    - Media duplication services

Restrictions:
Associated print services are only available as part of the overall print management service and not separately. Command and house paper services require engagement with HH Global early in the process. The framework runs for 4 years, with customer call-off contracts up to 4 years starting any time before the agreement ends. Call-off contracts can be full-term or initial period with options to extend.
