**Framework Name**: Occupational Health and Related Services DPS

**Framework Number**: RM6296

**Category**: HR and Workforce Services

**Objective**: Provide access to preventative services and treatments supporting employee physical and mental health and well-being for UK public sector bodies, the wider public sector, and third-sector organisations.

**Scope**:
- Eligible sectors: Public sector bodies, wider public sector, third-sector organisations
- Service types:
  - Full service providers (covering all occupational health and employee assistance programmes)
  - Occupational health services
  - Employee assistance programmes
  - Eye care and hearing services
  - Health surveillance services
  - Health screening services
  - Vaccinations
  - Guidance and counselling services
  - Rehabilitation services

**Restrictions**: Available to use until 27 October 2028. Complies with Public Contracts Regulations (PCR) 2015. More information at: [RM6296 Agreement](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6296).
