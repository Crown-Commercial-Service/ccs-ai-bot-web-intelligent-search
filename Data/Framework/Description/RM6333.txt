**Framework Name:** International Healthcare Professional Recruitment and Associated Services  
**Framework Number:** RM6333  
**Category:** HR and Workforce Services  

**Objective:**  
Facilitate the recruitment of healthcare professionals from overseas by public sector organizations using experienced international recruitment organizations. Support ethical and sustainable international recruitment in alignment with the NHS Long Term Plan. Enhance workforce capacity in critical healthcare and administrative roles.

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible Sectors:** All public sector organizations  
- **Service Types:**  
  - Recruitment of nursing and midwifery, including health visitors and dental nursing  
  - Recruitment for personal social services, including social workers and residential healthcare  
  - Recruitment for administrative roles, including human resources and finance  
  - Recruitment of allied health professionals, including clinical psychologists and physiotherapists  
  - Recruitment for emergency services, including ambulance workers  

Restrictions (if any)
