Framework Name: Adult Skills and Learning DPS  
Framework Number: RM6348  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  

Objective: 
This framework provides access to a range of adult training services funded by public money. The training aims to help adults and young adults develop skills necessary for employment, apprenticeship preparation, or further qualifications.  

Scope: 
- Eligible sectors: All public sector organizations  
- Service types:  
  - Post 19 skills  
  - Post 16 skills  
  - Adult skills fund  
  - Free courses for jobs  
  - UK Shared Prosperity Fund  
  - Multiply  
  - Skills Bootcamps  
  - Future iterations of existing funding types  
  - Any future funding types  

Restrictions: 
- Apprenticeship levy training is excluded. For this, refer to the Apprenticeship Training Dynamic Marketplace.  
- For corporate training of public sector employees, refer to the Learning and Training Services DPS.  
- This DPS is available until October 27, 2028, with no extension possible.  
- Compliance with Public Contracts Regulations (PCR) 2015 is required.  

For more information, visit [Adult Skills and Learning DPS RM6348](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6348). Watch this [video overview](URL LINK).
