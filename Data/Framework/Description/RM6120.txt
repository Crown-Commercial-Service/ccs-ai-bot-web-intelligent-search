Framework Name: Leasing and Loan Finance DPS  
Framework Number: RM6120  
Category: Financial Services  

Objective:  
This DPS facilitates access to funding for government and public sector bodies seeking to acquire assets. It provides a variety of financing options, including leasing and asset-secured loans. It connects customers to a range of lenders for efficient asset acquisition financing.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors:
  - Central government
  - Ministry of Defence
  - Local authorities
  - Health
  - Education
  - Other public sectors

- Service Types:
  - Hire purchase (asset ownership after installment payment completion)
  - Finance lease (lender retains legal ownership during lease)
  - Operating lease (temporary asset use without ownership transfer)
  - Sale and leaseback (asset is sold and leased back from the buyer)
  - Asset-secured loans (asset used as collateral)

Restrictions:  
Fleet financing requiring Contract Hire must use the Vehicle Lease, Fleet Management & Flexible Rental Solutions agreement (RM6096). Property financing is not eligible. Asset value must be a minimum of £2000, with no maximum limit applied.

For further details, refer to the agreement specification through the provided URL: [Leasing and Loan Finance DPS RM6120](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6120).
