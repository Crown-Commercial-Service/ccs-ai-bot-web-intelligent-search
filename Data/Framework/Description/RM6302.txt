Framework Name: Language Services  
Framework Number: RM6302  
Category: Professional Services  
Objective: Provides innovative language services designed to meet the public sector’s core needs, including translation, transcription, and interpreting. Employs technological solutions like Machine Translation and Automated Speech Recognition for efficient service delivery across the UK.  

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Public sector, Third sector
- Service types: 
  - Translation (written, spoken, and visual)
  - Transcription (including stenography and real-time transcription)
  - Interpreting (spoken, British Sign Language, remote, and overseas)

Restrictions:
- Lot 2 does not support visual or tactile requirements like Braille or In-vision BSL.
