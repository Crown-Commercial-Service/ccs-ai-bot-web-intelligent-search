Framework Name: Quality Assurance and Testing for IT Systems 2

Framework Number: RM6148

Category: Digital and Technology Services

Objective: To provide independent quality assurance testing (QAT) services for new digital systems and services to ensure they are suitable for public launch. It leverages a Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS) to facilitate the procurement of these services across the public sector and their associated bodies and agencies.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Whole public sector
- Service types:
  - Testing environments
  - Specialists
  - Automation
  - Consultancy services

Restrictions (if any):
None indicated
