Framework Name: Campaign Solutions 2  
Framework Number: RM6125  
Category: Professional Services  

Objective:
UK government and public sector bodies can access services needed for end-to-end campaign solutions to support public service campaigns.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: UK government and public sector bodies
- Service types: 
  - Creative, partnerships, marketing, and PR
  - Media strategy and planning 
  - Fulfillment services such as translation, transcreation, and versioning 
  - Stock imagery, footage, and sound 
  - Major or strategically important events 

Restrictions:
This agreement runs for up to 4 years with a review after the initial 2-year period. Campaign Solutions 2 is complemented by the Communications Marketplace agreement (DPS) for specialist expertise and niche marketing and communication services.
