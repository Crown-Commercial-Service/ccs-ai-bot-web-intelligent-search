Framework Name: Courier and Specialist Movements  
Framework Number: RM6171  
Category: Facilities Management  
Objective: Provides all UK public sector customers with access to a comprehensive range of courier and specialist movement services, from simple parcel deliveries to complex, large-scale specialist services. The framework is designed to replace RM3798 Courier Services and RM3799 Specialist Courier Services and is structured into six lots, ensuring extensive collection and delivery options.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Public sector
- Service types: Parcel deliveries, document transport, bulk pallet movements, high-value secure items, samples, specimens, pharmaceutical products (including controlled drugs and medication), firearms, weapons and ammunition
- Additional services: Asset recovery for disposal and auction of obsolete items (includes vehicles, IT, and office equipment)

Restrictions: Agreement valid for four years, expiring on 10 August 2025.
