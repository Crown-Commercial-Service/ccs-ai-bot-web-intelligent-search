**Framework Name:** Cloud Compute 2  
**Framework Number:** RM6292  
**Category:** Cloud and Hosting  

**Objective:**  
Cloud Compute 2 aims to provide central government and wider public sector organizations with access to comprehensive cloud computing services, including Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS). The framework facilitates both direct and reseller-supported access to core and ancillary cloud services to support various organizational needs.

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible sectors:** Central government, wider public sector organizations  
- **Service types:**  
  - **Core services:**
    - Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS)
    - Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS)
    - Microservices for usage, security, governance, optimization, and management  
  - **Ancillary services:**
    - Optimized billing and administration
    - 'FinOps' tooling
  - **Professional support services:**
    - Consultancy and enterprise support for cloud planning, adoption, or maximization

**Restrictions:**  
This agreement will run from 28 November 2023 for 3 years and will end in 2027, with the option to extend by up to 1 year.
