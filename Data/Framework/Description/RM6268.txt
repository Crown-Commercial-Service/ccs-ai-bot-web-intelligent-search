**Framework Name:** Vehicle Lease, Fleet Management and Salary Sacrifice Car Schemes  
**Framework Number:** RM6268  
**Category:** Fleet  

**Objective:**  
This framework enables central government and the wider public sector to lease a variety of passenger cars and commercial vehicles. It includes provisions for fleet management and salary sacrifice car schemes. The goal is to facilitate access to a range of vehicles, including low-emission options, and offer comprehensive fleet management services.

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible sectors:** Central government, wider public sector  
- **Service types:**
  - Leasing passenger cars and commercial vehicles (light, medium, heavy)
  - Fleet management services
  - Salary sacrifice car schemes
  - Access to all fuel types (electric, hybrid, hydrogen)
  - Quotes through Fleet Portal
  - Sourcing vehicles (leased, purchased, hired)
  - Fleet administration tasks
  - Creation of fleet policies
  - Support for transitioning to low-emission vehicles
  - Driver training and risk assessment

**Restrictions (if any):**  
None specified.

For more information, visit the [framework page](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6268).
