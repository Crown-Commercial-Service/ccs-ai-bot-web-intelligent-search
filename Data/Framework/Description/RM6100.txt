**Framework Name:** Technology Services 3  
**Framework Number:** RM6100  
**Category:** Digital and Technology Services  

**Objective:**  
Provides access to technology strategy and service design, as well as services supporting the operational deployment of IT estates. This agreement supports large projects up to top secret classification and offers various technology services to public sector customers.  

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible Sectors:** All public sector customers  
- **Service Types:**  
  - Provision and management of IT service desk  
  - End user device support  
  - Network support  
  - Asset disposal and application maintenance  
  - IT infrastructure support (e.g., server and storage hardware)  

**Restrictions:**  
- Duration: 4 years with no extension options  
- Contracts: Up to 7 years depending on the lot  

For more information, visit [Technology Services 3 RM6100](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6100).
