Framework Name: Demand Management & Renewables

Framework Number: RM6314

Category: Construction

Objective: Provides central government and the wider public sector with a range of decarbonisation products and services to support their carbon net zero strategy. Enables you to access a wide range of products and services to reduce your energy demands and support your organisation's decarbonisation targets.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government, wider public sector
- Service types:
  - Energy products (solar photovoltaic technology, heat pumps, battery storage units)
  - Design and feasibility services
  - Installation services
  - Servicing and maintenance services
  - Decommissioning services

Restrictions: 
- No maximum contract length
- Agreement duration of 4 years, ending on 23 March 2027, with no options for extension

For further details, please visit [Demand Management & Renewables Framework: RM6314](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6314).
