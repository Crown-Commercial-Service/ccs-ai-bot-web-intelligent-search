Framework Name: Insourced Services to Support the Provision of Healthcare (including Clinical Insourcing)

Framework Number: RM6276

Category: HR and Workforce Services

Objective:
NHS and other public sector organisations can procure insourced services to deliver comprehensive healthcare services, ensuring efficiency and cost-effectiveness. This includes solutions for various medical specialties such as diagnostics, cardiology, ophthalmology, and physiotherapy carried out on-site using existing equipment.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: 
  - NHS foundation trusts
  - NHS trusts
  - Special health authorities
  - Acute sector (e.g., hospitals)
  - Mental health settings
  - Community health and ambulance services
  - Care trusts
  - Clinical Commissioning Groups
  - Integrated Care Systems
  - Community Interest Companies
  - GPs

- Service Types:
  - Cardiology diagnostics
  - Diagnostic imaging
  - Endoscopy
  - Ear, nose, and throat treatments
  - General surgery
  - Gynaecology
  - Ophthalmology
  - Orthopaedics
  - Physiotherapy
  - Urology
  - Full or partial patient pathway management

Restrictions:
- Initial agreement period of 2 years with options for two 1-year extensions.
