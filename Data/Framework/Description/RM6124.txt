**Framework Name**: Communications Marketplace  
**Framework Number**: RM6124  
**Category**: Professional Services  

**Objective**:  
A Marketplace for marketing and communications services to assist UK government and public sector bodies in running public service campaigns. It provides access to a broad range of specialist services from agencies in the UK and overseas, facilitated through a Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS).

**Scope**:  
- Eligible sectors: UK government and public sector organizations  
- Service types:  
  - Creative development  
  - Direct marketing  
  - Sponsorship  
  - Events  
  - Production  
  - Public Relations (PR)  
  - Social marketing and emerging channels  
  - Campaign coordination  
  - Marketing and advertising technology advice  
  - Data science  
  - In-house agency services  
  - Recruitment advertising  
  - Partnerships  
  - Content aggregation marketing  
  - Marketing and communications strategy  
  - Marketing strategic advice and consultancy  
  - Data provision, management, and augmentation  
  - Internal communications and engagement  
  - Innovation and experience  

This agreement enables direct relationships with agencies of all sizes offering specialist expertise and niche services. Agencies can apply to join the Marketplace anytime. The Communications Marketplace complements the Campaign Solutions 2 (RM6125) framework, which provides longer-term, strategic relationships for comprehensive campaign solutions.
