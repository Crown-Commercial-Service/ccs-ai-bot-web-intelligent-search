Framework Name: Construction Professional Services  
Framework Number: RM6165  
Category: Construction  

Objective:
Central government and the wider public sector can access a wide range of technical advisory services supporting building and civil engineering projects of all scales and sizes. The flexible contract structure is designed to provide solutions specific to user needs while promoting collaborative working practices between the industry and end users.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government, wider public sector, arm’s length bodies
- Service types: 
  - Project management
  - Cost management
  - Engineering (civil, structural, electrical)
  - Architectural and design services for buildings, spaces, and infrastructure
  - Environmental and sustainability services (e.g., carbon neutral efficiencies)

Restrictions (if any): None specified.  
URL link: [Construction Professional Services: RM6165](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6165)

This agreement supports key government policies and strategies including the construction playbook, building information modelling (BIM), lifecycle cost, prompt payment, social value, and carbon net-zero initiatives. The framework is valid for four years.
