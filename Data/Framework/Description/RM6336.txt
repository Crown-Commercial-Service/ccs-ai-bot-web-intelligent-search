Framework Name: eDisclosure and Review Services 2  
Framework Number: RM6336  
Category: Professional Services  

Objective:  
The eDisclosure and Review Services 2 framework provides disclosure services in alignment with the Electronic Discovery Reference Model. It aims to assist central government and the wider public sector with managing electronically-stored information in litigation and regulatory compliance scenarios. This agreement allows flexibility to opt for complete services or select specific elements of the disclosure process.  

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors:  
  - Central government  
  - Wider public sector  
- Service Types:  
  - Identification  
  - Preservation  
  - Collection  
  - Processing  
  - Review  
  - Analysis  
  - Production  
  - Presentation at trial  

Restrictions:  
- The agreement expires on 6 March 2027.
