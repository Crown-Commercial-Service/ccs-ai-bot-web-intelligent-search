Framework Name: Restructuring & Insolvency Services  
Framework Number: RM6269  
Category: Professional Services  
Objective: UK government and public sector bodies can access specialist restructuring and insolvency services from a range of suppliers in a cost-effective way. Central government departments can utilize these services during instances of corporate distress where there is a governmental interest.  

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Government and public sector bodies  
- Service types: 
  - Accelerated mergers and acquisitions
  - Business review
  - Cash-flow review
  - Distressed debt restructuring
  - General restructuring advice
  - Insolvency contingency planning
  - Options analysis

Restrictions (if any): The agreement will last for 3 years, with an option to extend for an additional 12 months. While there is no defined maximum length for a call-off agreement under this framework, it is recommended that call-offs should not exceed 4 years.
