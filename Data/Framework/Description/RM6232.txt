**Framework Name: Facilities Management and Workplace Services**  
**Framework Number: RM6232**  
**Category: Facilities Management**  
**Objective: This agreement provides public sector organizations in the UK with access to a comprehensive range of facilities management services. Suppliers offer extensive geographical coverage based on NUTS regions and can potentially serve international requirements related to UK projects.**

**Scope:**
- **Eligible Sectors: Public Sector Organizations**
- **Service Types:**
  - Contract management and mobilisation
  - Social value and Carbon Net Zero initiatives
  - Maintenance services for business assets
  - Statutory obligations like portable appliance testing
  - Landscaping and catering services
  - Routine and reactive cleaning services, pest control
  - Workplace FM and visitor support services
  - Security and waste management services
  - Defence FM services and property management
  - Computer Aided Facility Management (CAFM) and helpdesk
  - Management of billable works
  
**Restrictions:** Consultants must sign a non-disclosure agreement and get organizational approval via a consultant approval form.
