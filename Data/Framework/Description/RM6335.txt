Framework Name: Digital & Legacy Application Services  
Framework Number: RM6335  
Category: Digital and Technology Services

Objective: Access suppliers who provide IT digital and legacy application services (DALAS) to support the rollout of future applications, reducing dependency on legacy technologies.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government departments, local authorities, health, police, fire and rescue, education, devolved administrations, and other UK public sector bodies
- Service types: IT digital services for developing and implementing future applications, transition to non-legacy application support models

Restrictions: None
