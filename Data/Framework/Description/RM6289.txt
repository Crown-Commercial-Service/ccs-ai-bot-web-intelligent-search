Framework Name: Provision of Power Purchase Agreement  
Framework Number: RM6289  
Category: Energy  

Objective:  
This framework provides central government and the wider public sector access to renewable electricity through long-term contracts with suppliers. It allows organizations to purchase renewable electricity directly from specific energy projects, aiding in meeting carbon net-zero targets.

Scope:  
- Eligible sectors: Central government, wider public sector  
- Service types: 
  - Group buying power through aggregation to secure competitive prices  
  - Long-term agreements, typically lasting between 5 and 15 years  
  - Flexible contract types primarily set up for pay-as-produced electricity  

Restrictions: N/A
