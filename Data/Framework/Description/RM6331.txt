Framework Name: Healthcare Soft FM  
Framework Number: RM6331  
Category: Facilities Management  

Objective:  
Buy soft facilities management services designed for the Healthcare sector. This framework includes services such as linen and laundry, catering, waste management, cleaning, and security. It reflects the latest NHS standards, including the National Standards of Healthcare Cleanliness 2021 and HTM 07-01 for waste management.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors: NHS acute trusts, community health trusts, mental health trusts, ambulance services, GP’s, all public sector, and central government bodies  
- Service Types:  
  - Linen and laundry (collection, wash, return, specialist services)  
  - Waste services (general, recycled, clinical, medical waste)  
  - Security (alarm response, access control, passes, perimeter guarding)  
  - Visitor support services (reception duties, telephony services)  
  - Grounds maintenance (soft landscaping, tree surgery, snow and ice clearance, planting)  
  - Porterage services (transporting patients, stores, equipment)  
  - Catering (patient feeding, meeting hospitality, event catering, restaurant services, vending services)  
  - Cleaning (routine, reactive cleaning, pest control, ambulance decontamination)  

Restrictions:  
- None specified
