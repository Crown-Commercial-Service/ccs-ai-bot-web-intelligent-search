Framework Name: Supply of Tyres, Glass and Fast Fit Solutions  
Framework Number: RM6142  
Category: Fleet  

Objective:  
This agreement provides the entire public sector access to services for the supply and fit of tyres, as well as the repair and replacement of vehicle glass. It also includes fast-fit products and services such as planned maintenance, MOTs, diagnostics, and repairs.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors: Entire public sector  
- Service Types:  
  - Supply and fit of tyres  
  - Supply and fit of vehicle glass repairs  
  - Fast fit solutions (bulbs, wiper blades, etc.)  
  - Repairs (brakes, exhausts, batteries)  
  - Servicing (MOTs, diagnostics)  
  - Chip repair service  

Restrictions:  
- This agreement will run for 4 years and will expire in July 2025.  
- No options to extend. 

For more information, visit: [Supply of Tyres, Glass and Fast Fit Solutions RM6142](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6142)
