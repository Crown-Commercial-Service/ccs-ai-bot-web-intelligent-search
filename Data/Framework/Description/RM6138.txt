**Framework Name:** Insurance Services 3 DPS  
**Framework Number:** RM6138  
**Category:** Financial Services  

**Objective:**  
To provide access to a complete range of suppliers for the placement of insurance policies of all classes, facilitating both broker-mediated and direct customer dealings. The DPS helps customers find relevant suppliers through a robust filtering system.

**Scope:**
- **Eligible Sectors:** Various sectors requiring insurance services
- **Service Types:**
  - Broker-mediated insurance placements
  - Direct customer insurance dealings
  - Geography-based services
  - Sector-specific services
  - Insurance class and subclass services
  - Associated additional services

**Restrictions:**  
Award of call-off contracts is through further competition only. This framework operates for 8 years and expires on 5 February 2028.
