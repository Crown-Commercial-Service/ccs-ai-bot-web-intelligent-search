Framework Name: Research & Insights  
Framework Number: RM6126  
Category: Professional Services  

Objective:  
The Research & Insights framework enables public sector organisations to access a comprehensive range of social, economic, and market research services, as well as behavioural insights. By leveraging this framework, organisations can gather valuable evidence, understand audiences, and develop informed policies. This DPS helps identify relevant suppliers based on specific needs through a filtering system.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: All public sector organisations
- Service types:  
  - Social, economic, and market research  
  - Behavioural insights  
  - Media monitoring, analysis, evaluation, and associated services  
  - Applied behavioural insights  
  
Restrictions:
- The initial agreement period is four years, with an optional extension for two years. If still suitable, the agreement can be further extended for two more years.
- Suppliers may apply to join the DPS at any time during the agreement's duration.
