**Framework Name:** Logistics, Warehousing and Supply Chain Solutions  
**Framework Number:** RM6329  
**Category:** Facilities Management  

**Objective:**  
This agreement provides a full range of logistics and warehousing solutions and services for collection, receipt, warehousing/storage, management, processing, and onward distribution. It is specifically designed for central government and wider public sector organisations.

**Scope:**  
- Eligible Sectors: Central government, Wider public sector organisations  
- Service Types:  
  - Fulfilment (managing orders from the point of sale to delivery)  
  - Large logistical movements including construction and vehicles  
  - Storage  
  - Removals and relocations  
  - Events and exhibitions  

**Restrictions:**  
None specified.  

For more information, visit the [agreement page](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6329).
