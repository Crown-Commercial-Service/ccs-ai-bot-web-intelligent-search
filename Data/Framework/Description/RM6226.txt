Framework Name: Debt Resolution Services  
Framework Number: RM6226  
Category: Financial Services  

Objective:  
Designed to provide ethical and fair outcomes for citizens in vulnerable situations, this agreement offers a range of debt recovery solutions and data services. It aims to align with the cross-government debt management strategy, ensuring efficient and effective use of taxpayer money.  

Scope:  
- Eligible sectors:  
  - Central government and their arm’s length bodies and agencies  
  - Wider public sector and third sector  
  - Local government  
  - Health and education  
  - Emergency services (police, fire, rescue)  
  - Housing associations and charities  
  
- Service types:  
  - Managed debt collection agencies  
  - Data and analytics solutions  
  - Enforcement agents (direct engagement)  
  - Managed enforcement agents (panel management)  
  - Litigation services  
  - Auctioneers  
  - Process servers  
  - Spend analysis and recovery services  

Restrictions:  
- This agreement will replace Debt Management Services (RM6208).  
- It will run for 4 years, expiring in December 2025.  
- Individual call-offs can be extended for up to 3 years beyond the agreement end date but no longer than 3 years after the agreement has ended.
