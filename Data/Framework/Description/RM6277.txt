Framework Name: Non Clinical Staffing  
Framework Number: RM6277  
Category: HR and Workforce Services

Objective: This framework enables public and third sector organizations to hire quality non-clinical workers through various engagement models, including fixed-term positions, temporary roles, and contractors. It aims to provide efficient staffing solutions for roles across diverse administrative and support services.

Scope: 
- Eligible Sectors:
  - NHS contracting authorities
  - Central government
  - Local government
  - Universities
  - Charities
  - Emergency (blue light) services

- Service Types:
  - Administration and secretarial roles
  - Finance, accounts, and audit roles
  - IT technicians, analysts, and digital workers
  - Legal secretaries, paralegals, and lawyers
  - Environmental and scientific roles (e.g., senior assistant scientists, clinical coders)
  - Catering, driving, and security roles
  - Estates and maintenance roles (e.g., electricians, surveyors)
  - Project management and managed services

Restrictions: Roles requiring medical qualifications are out of scope and can be sourced through the Clinical Staffing agreement.

For more information, visit: [Non Clinical Staffing RM6277](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6277)
