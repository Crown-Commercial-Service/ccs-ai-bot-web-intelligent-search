**Framework Name:**
Income Generation from Estates, Assets & IP DPS

**Framework Number:**
RM6350

**Category:**
Professional Services

**Objective:**
This agreement enables government and public sector organisations to generate income from their estates, assets, and intellectual property through various means such as vending machines, automated self-service, cash points, mobile device charging, delivery lockers, brand licensing and sponsorship, advertising, and other services. It utilizes a Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS) with a filtering system to identify a shortlist of relevant suppliers, allowing suppliers to join at any time.

**Scope:**
- Eligible sectors: Central government, agencies, arms-length bodies, publicly owned transport organisations, local authorities, non-departmental public bodies, airports, schools, devolved administrations, charities.
- Service types:
  - Supply and management of vending machines (traditional and non-traditional)
  - Automated retail and pop-up retail
  - Photo booths
  - Reverse vending solutions (deposit return schemes)
  - Cash machines (ATMs)
  - Portable battery chargers
  - Delivery/storage lockers
  - IP licensing and sponsorship of fixed and non-fixed assets
  - Static and digital advertising (large and small format)

**Restrictions (if any):**
None indicated.
