Framework Name: National Fuels 3  
Framework Number: RM6305  
Category: Energy  

Objective: National Fuels 3 provides public sector customers access to liquid fuels, heating oil, and automotive fuels. It also includes services associated with fuel usage such as biomass, tank maintenance, and energy bureau services.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: UK public sector organizations, including central government departments and their agencies, the wider public sector, and charities  
- Service types: 
  - Heating oil (kerosene)  
  - Transport fuel (diesel and petrol)  
  - Liquid petroleum gas (LPG) in cylinders  
  - Liquified natural gas (LNG)  
  - Biomass  
  - Fuel tank replacement  
  - Fuel tank maintenance  
  - Fuel testing

Restrictions:
- Suppliers provide fuel by regional postcodes (lots 1A to 1N) and a national lot (1O)
- 4-year duration with no extension option
- Contracts can run for up to 4 years from the start date
