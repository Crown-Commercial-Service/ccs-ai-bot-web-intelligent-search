Framework Name: Supply of Energy 2
Framework Number: RM6251
Category: Energy

Objective:
Central government and wider public sector organizations can access electricity and gas through a specialist risk-managed energy procurement service. The dedicated energy trading and risk management team will buy and administer electricity and gas on behalf of the users.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: Central government, wider public sector organizations
- Service Types:
  - Supply of gas and electricity managed by CCS expert in-house energy trading team
  - Access to a range of energy trading baskets to suit various demand and risk levels
  - Provisions for sleeving power purchase agreements (PPAs) for green energy
  - Access to clean ‘zero carbon’ energy
  - Dedicated customer service support
  - Carbon offsetting credits and allowances
  - Data and portfolio management
  - Siteworks and metering services
  - Support with carbon reporting
  - Additional services as required

Restrictions: This is a 4-year framework agreement.
