Framework Name: Workforce Solutions  
Framework Number: RM6288  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  
Objective: Workforce Solutions provides public sector organisations with access to professional, managerial, operational, and administrative contractors and temporary workers through a single managed service provider (MSP). The framework aims to help organisations meet their business goals and workforce needs effectively.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All public sector organisations
- Service Types:
  - Temporary worker sourcing (hourly/daily rates)
  - Statements of work (SOW)
  - Recruit-train-deploy (RTD)
  - Permanent recruitment (fixed-term and internal secondment roles)
  - Resource augmentation partnering (employed resource model)
  - Recruitment campaign services (later date addition)

Restrictions:
- Permanent recruitment, resource augmentation partnering, and campaign services will not be available at the agreement start.
- The agreement will run for 6 years, with an 18-month transition period during which new customers cannot join but existing contracts can continue.
