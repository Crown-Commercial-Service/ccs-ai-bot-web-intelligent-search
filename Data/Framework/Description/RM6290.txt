Framework Name: Executive & Non-Executive Recruitment Services  
Framework Number: RM6290  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  

Objective: This framework allows all public sector organizations to access executive and non-executive recruitment services for permanent, fixed-term, and inward secondment roles. Services are offered on a modular basis, enabling the purchase of only the needed services. Core services can also be accessed as part of an end-to-end recruitment solution.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All public sector organizations across the UK, including England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.
- Service Types: Core services (identification, assessment and evaluation, offer and appointment, customer planning); Non-core services (strategy and planning, talent development, talent mapping, reserve lists and talent pools, executive development and coaching, technology services, international recruitment).

Restrictions: 
None
