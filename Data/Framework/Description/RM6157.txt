Framework Name: Building Materials and Equipment
Framework Number: RM6157
Category: Construction

Objective: This framework offers UK government departments, devolved administrations, and the wider public sector access to a range of building materials, equipment, and associated services to support construction projects. It aims to promote collaborative working practices by integrating industry and end-users.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: UK government departments, devolved administrations, wider public sector
- Service types:
  * Heavy and general building materials
  * Heating and electrical products
  * Domestic kitchens
  * Paints and solvents
  * Flooring
  * Tool and plant sale and hire
  * Protective wear

Restrictions: Terms and conditions are governed by the Framework Alliance Contract (FAC-1), which supports merging teams into an alliance to deliver improved results.

Further details available [here](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6157).

Note: This framework is part of the CCS integrated strategy for construction, which includes three other agreements: Construction Professional Services (RM6165), Modular Buildings (RM6014), and Construction Works and Associated Services (RM6088).
