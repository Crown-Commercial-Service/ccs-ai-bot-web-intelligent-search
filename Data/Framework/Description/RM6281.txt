National Framework for the Provision of Clinical and Healthcare Staffing  
Framework Number: RM6281  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  

Objective: This framework aims to facilitate the temporary recruitment of clinical and healthcare staff for all job roles and bands across the UK. It ensures a streamlined and approved process by NHS England. The agreement supports public sector bodies in accessing qualified temporary and fixed-term workers to address staffing needs in healthcare settings.

Scope:  
- Eligible sectors: All public sector bodies across the UK
- Service types:
  - Nursing and midwifery
  - Medical and dental
  - Allied health professionals
  - Health science services
  - Emergency services
  - Social care

Restrictions: None
