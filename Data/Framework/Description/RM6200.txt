Framework Name: Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Framework Number: RM6200
Category: Software

Objective:
The aim of this framework is to provide discovery and consultancy services, implementation and support, and end-to-end partnerships in the field of artificial intelligence (AI) for public sector organizations and their associated bodies and agencies. It offers a Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS) to help organizations at various stages of AI adoption procure relevant services and technologies.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: health and social care, blue light services, central government, devolved administrations, local government, and not-for-profit organizations.
- Service types include: 
  - AI software applications
  - Machine learning for data analytics
  - Intelligent virtual assistants and personal assistants
  - Medical imaging software
  - Non-imaging diagnostic software
  - Symptom-based software

Restrictions:
Contracts under this framework can range up to four years, adjusted according to the complexity of the project.
