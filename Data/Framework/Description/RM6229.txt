Framework Name: Permanent Recruitment 2  
Framework Number: RM6229  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  
Objective: Provide recruitment services for public sector organizations to hire permanent, fixed-term, and internal secondment roles. Offer clinical and non-clinical recruitment services across the UK through a competitive, non-exclusive contingency model.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All public sector organizations, including NHS customers.
- Service Types: 
  - Core Services:
    - Search services to find applicants
    - Evaluation of applicants
    - Appointment of applicant to the role
  - Non-Core Services:
    - Strategy and planning services for recruitment gaps
    - Talent development services for building a talent pool

Restrictions:
- Agreement runs for 2 years, with an option to extend for up to 2 additional years.
- Recommend call-off contracts to not exceed 4 years.
