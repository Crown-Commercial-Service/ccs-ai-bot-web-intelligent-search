Framework Name: Managed Staff Banks  
Framework Number: RM6278  
Category: HR and Workforce Services  

Objective: This framework provides options for managed outsourced staff bank services aimed primarily at the NHS but open to all public sector bodies, including central government. It offers two service models to manage any or all elements of staff bank operations. The aim is to help businesses address staffing gaps, manage workforce effectively, and improve shift fill rates.  

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors: NHS, public sector bodies, central government  
- Service Types:  
  - Internal and external recruitment of bank workers  
  - Ensuring compliance with NHS employment check standards  
  - Payroll management  
  - Performance management services  
  - Management of agency cascade for unfilled positions  

Restrictions:  
Contracts can be let for any period, typically 3 to 4 years, depending on workforce strategy. Call-off contracts will continue to run their full course even after the agreement ends.
