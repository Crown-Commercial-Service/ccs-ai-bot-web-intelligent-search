Framework Name: Vertical Application Solutions  
Framework Number: RM6259  
Category: Software  

Objective: Public sector customers can access software-focused solutions tailored to their specific industry, business application, or service needs. Solutions include software licenses, associated hardware, app-related consultancy services, software support, and maintenance. This agreement supersedes Data and Application Solutions (DAS) and caters to various public sector entities offering comprehensive software sourcing options.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors:  
  - Government departments and their arm’s length bodies  
  - Devolved administrations  
  - Wider public sector  
  - Third sector organizations  
  - Particular consideration for blue light (emergency services), education, local authority, and social care sectors  

- Service Types:  
  - Implementation, testing, and provision of a range of software solutions  
  - Access to solution-essential hardware and professional services  
  - Purchase of cloud-hosted and on-premise software, or a combination of both  
  - Direct award using digital catalog or further competition depending on complexity  

Restrictions:  
- Initial duration: 30 months (2.5 years)
- Option to extend: Additional 18 months (1.5 years)
- No maximum contract length for call-off contracts.
