Framework Name: Buying Better Food and Drink  
Framework Number: RM6279  
Category: Facilities Management  

Objective: 
This agreement allows public sector organizations to procure food and drink through a single online access point, ensuring high-quality, regulatory-compliant products. It supports sustainability, quality, and welfare standards, in line with Government Buying Standards for Food and Catering (GBSF). The framework also aims to reduce waste, food miles, and modern slavery risks.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors: Government, local authorities, charities, non-profits  
- Service Types: 
  - Food and drink procurement
  - Sustainability and social value initiatives
  - Support for SMEs and local suppliers

Restrictions:  
- Initial term of 2 years, with an option to extend for two additional 1 year periods  
- Single supplier agreement with direct award call-offs  
- Maximum call-off length of 12 months
