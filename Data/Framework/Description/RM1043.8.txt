Framework Name: Digital Outcomes 6  
Framework Number: RM1043.8  
Category: Digital and Technology Services  

Objective:  
A route to market to access agile development and user-centred design services to accelerate innovation within the public sector. Find suppliers who can design and build bespoke digital products and services using an agile approach. 

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government, Charities, Education, Health, Local authority, Blue light services (police, fire, ambulance, search and rescue), Devolved administrations
- Service types: Agile development, User-centred design, Studio space for user research, Access to users for service testing

Restrictions (if any):  
The agreement will run for 24 months, with an optional 12-month extension. The agreement will run alongside RM6263 Digital Specialists and Programmes and will replace RM1043.7 Digital Outcomes and Specialists 5.
