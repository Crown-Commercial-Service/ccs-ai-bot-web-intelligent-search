**Framework Name:** Demand Management & Renewables DPS  
**Framework Number:** RM6313  
**Category:** Construction  

**Objective:**  
Supports public sector carbon net zero strategies by providing a range of energy products and services at different stages of energy projects. The framework offers an end-to-end energy service that includes design, implementation, and maintenance.

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible Sectors:** Public sector  
- **Service Types:**  
  - Design and feasibility services  
  - Installation services  
  - Servicing and maintenance services  
  - Buy-only options (e.g., solar photovoltaic panels)  
  - Decommissioning services  
  - Policy delivery  
  - End-to-end service  

- **Industry Areas:**  
  - Solar photovoltaic  
  - L.E.D Lighting  
  - Hydrogen for heat  
  - Heat network delivery  
  - Heat pumps  
  - Energy bureau services  
  - Metering services  

**Restrictions:**  
- Suppliers must complete a Financial Viability Risk Assessment and provide the latest statement of accounts.  
- Contract awards can extend past the end date of the agreement.  

For more details, visit [Demand Management & Renewables DPS RM6313](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6313).
