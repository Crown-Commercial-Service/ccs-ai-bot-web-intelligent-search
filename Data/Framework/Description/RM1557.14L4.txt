**Framework Name**: G-Cloud 14 Lot 4  
**Framework Number**: RM1557.14L4  
**Category**: Cloud and Hosting  

**Objective**:  
Public sector customers can access comprehensive cloud support services, including set up, migration, security, and performance testing, through further competition from multiple categories under this agreement. 

**Scope**:  
- Eligible sectors: Public sector  
- Service types:  
  - Cloud migration planning  
  - Set up and migration  
  - Security services  
  - Quality assurance and performance testing  
  - Training  
  - Ongoing support  

**Restrictions**:  
- The framework will run for 18 months from 29 October 2024.  
- Call-off contracts initially last up to 36 months with a possible 12-month extension.  
- Total call-off length not to exceed 48 months.  
- Specific restrictions apply to central government contract extensions.
