Framework Name: Furniture and Associated Services 2
Framework Number: RM6308
Category: Facilities Management
Objective: To enable public sector organizations to purchase furniture for diverse settings, including offices, residential areas, and educational institutions. It supports sustainable practices through repair, renovation, and the procurement of second-life furniture. The framework also provides design and space planning services tailored to various sectors.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government, local authorities, charities, not-for-profit organizations.
- Service types: Office furniture, residential furniture, educational furniture, heavy-duty racking and steel storage for operational environments, libraries, museums; renovation and repair services; second-life furniture.
- Includes: Design and space planning, site surveying, sustainability advice and guidance.

Restrictions: The framework has an initial term of 3 years, with the option to extend for an additional year.
