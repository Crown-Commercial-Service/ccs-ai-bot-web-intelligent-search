**Framework Name**: Offsite Construction Solutions  
**Framework Number**: RM6184  
**Category**: Construction  

**Objective**:  
Provide an efficient and cost-effective solution for offsite construction needs, offering both 3D volumetric modular and 2D panelised building methods. Enable public sector organizations to access full 'turn key' packages, from design to maintenance of prefabricated buildings. Ensure reduced disruption and lead time through offsite manufacturing and local or national delivery.

**Scope**:  
- **Eligible Sectors**: Public sector organizations  
- **Service Types**:  
  - Design, fabrication, and physical delivery of offsite construction solutions  
  - Construction or installation of prefabricated buildings  
  - Maintenance of prefabricated buildings  
  - Both permanent and temporary buildings

**Restrictions**:  
- There is no maximum contract length.  
- Agreement runs for 4 years until 23 January 2027, with an option to extend by 3 years until 23 January 2030.  
- Access to DFE approved designs/drawings for classrooms in lot 1 or its sub-lots requires an email request to offsite@crowncommercial.gov.uk
