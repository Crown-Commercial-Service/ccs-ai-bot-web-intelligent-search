**Framework Name:** Water, Wastewater and Ancillary Services 3  
**Framework Number:** RM6306  
**Category:** Energy  

**Objective:**  
This framework agreement provides retail services for water supply and wastewater (sewerage), along with essential ancillary services. It helps organizations manage water billing, improve customer service, and explore optional services that reduce water usage, save money, and lower carbon emissions.

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible Sectors:** Public sector organizations with non-residential premises in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, including schools, NHS trusts, and local authorities.  
- **Service Types:**
  - Managed water billing
  - Customer service
  - Optional services for water conservation, cost savings, and carbon reduction

**Restrictions:**
- Organizations served by a water supplier in Wales can only switch water retailers if they use more than 50 million litres (ML) of water a year.
  - If you use more than 50 ML a year: you can choose to switch retailer or stay with your current supplier.
  - If you use 50 ML or less a year: you will continue to get services from your regional water company and cannot switch using this framework.
