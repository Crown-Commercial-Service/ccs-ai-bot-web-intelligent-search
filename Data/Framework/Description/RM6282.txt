Framework Name: Storage, Distribution, Kitting and Associated Services
Framework Number: RM6282
Category: Facilities Management 
Objective: Access a range of storage, distribution, and kitting solutions under one agreement. Includes UK and international services, quality control of items, and specialist collection and delivery services.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: 
  - Central government departments
  - Local authorities
  - Emergency services (health, police, fire and rescue)
  - Education sector
  - Devolved administrations
- Service types: 
  - International air, rail, and sea freight services
  - Quality control of items
  - Asset tracking
  - UK and international storage, fulfilment, transport, and distribution
  - Specialist collection and delivery (medical samples, specimens, pharmaceuticals)
  - Recycling and disposal services
  - Print services (brochures)
  - Packaging (cardboard boxes, tamper proof seals, plastic mailing bags, medical packaging like absorbent pads)

Restrictions: The framework is split across 7 lots. It has no extensions and will expire on 14 February 2026.

For further information, visit: [RM6282 Agreement](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6282)
