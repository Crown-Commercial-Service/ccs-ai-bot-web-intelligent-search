Framework Name: Office Solutions  
Framework Number: RM6299  
Category: Facilities Management  

Objective:  
All UK public sector organisations can use this agreement to procure a range of office supplies and equipment, promoting efficient, cost-effective office management.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors:  
  - Central government departments and their agencies  
  - The wider public sector  
  - Charities and not for profit organisations  
  - Emergency services  
  - Education sector  
  - Health sector  
  - Local authorities  
- Service Types:  
  - Stationery and paper products  
  - Janitorial products including washroom supplies, washing-up liquid, and refuse sacks  
  - Standard and ergonomic office furniture  
  - Ergonomic equipment and supplies  
  - Electronic office supplies (EOS) such as fax, ink, and printer toner cartridges  
  - Small office machines, technology monitors, and headsets  

Restrictions (if any):  
- Duration: 3 years with an option to extend by 12 months  
- The call-off contract period is flexible and may extend beyond the framework expiry date based on the organisation’s commercial decision  
- This agreement replaces RM6059 Office Supplies which expired on 5 April 2024
