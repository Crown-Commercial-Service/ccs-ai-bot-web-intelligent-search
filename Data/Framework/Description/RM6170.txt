Framework Name: Print Management Services  
Framework Number: RM6170  
Category: Hardware  

Objective:  
To provide fully managed end-to-end print products and services, including bulk printing, direct mail, and secure print services, to all public sector organizations across the UK.

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All public sector organizations
- Service Types:
  - Command and house paper services (Lot 1 only)
  - Bulk printing
  - Full range of print products (envelopes, labels, folders)
  - Operational print (brochures, leaflets, business cards, business stationary)
  - Finishing services (binding, stamping, embossing, laminating)
  - Direct mail and transactional print services
  - Alternative formats (easy read, large print, alternative languages, audio, braille)
  - Secure print services and items
  - Print stock management and distribution
  - Pre-production services (content creation, digital asset management, typesetting, proofreading, translation)
  - Digital solutions and transformation services (email, SMS, website)
  - Election print and election mailing services
  - Associated print services (scanning, microfiche, secure shredding, media duplication, part of overall print management service)

Restrictions:
- Services under RM6071 Print Marketplace framework recommended for customers not needing print management support.
- Command and house paper services only available through Lot 1.
- Services not designed to be used separately but as part of an overall print management service.  
- Agreement duration: 4 years.  
- Customer call-off contracts: Up to 4 years, may include extensions (e.g., 2+1+1 years).
