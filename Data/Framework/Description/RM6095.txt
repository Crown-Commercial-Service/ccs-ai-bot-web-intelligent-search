Framework Name: Gigabit Capable Connectivity DPS  
Framework Number: RM6095  
Category: Network Services  

Objective: The Gigabit Capable Connectivity DPS aims to ensure UK public sector bodies have access to high-quality, gigabit-capable network services. It facilitates the procurement and supply of broadband connectivity and the infrastructure build necessary for such connectivity.  

Scope: 
- Eligible Sectors: All UK public sector bodies.
- Service Types:
  - Broadband connectivity services: gigabit-capable internet connectivity.
  - Infrastructure build services: construction of connectivity infrastructure.
  - Managed infrastructure services: connection maintenance between specific sites.

Restrictions: 
- Location-based filtering.
- Accreditation and Standards filtering: Public Services Network (PSN), Health and Social Care Network (HSCN), Cyber Essentials Plus.
