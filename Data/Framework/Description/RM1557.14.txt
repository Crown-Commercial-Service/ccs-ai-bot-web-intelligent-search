Framework Name: G-Cloud 14  
Framework Number: RM1557.14  
Category: Cloud and Hosting  

Objective:  
G-Cloud 14 provides an online catalogue for public sector organisations and charities to procure cloud-based computing services, including hosting, software, cloud support, and many off-the-shelf, pay-as-you-go cloud solutions. It aims to deliver a wide variety of cloud services from a range of suppliers and has replaced G-Cloud 13.

Scope:  
* Eligible sectors:
  - Central government
  - Charities
  - Education
  - Health
  - Local authority
  - Blue light services (police, fire, ambulance, search and rescue)
  - Devolved administrations
  - British overseas territories

* Service types:
  - Cloud hosting
  - Cloud software
  - Cloud support

Restrictions:  
The framework will run for 18 months starting from 29 October 2024. Call-off contracts can last up to 36 months, with an option to extend once by up to 12 months, not exceeding a total of 48 months. Extensions for central government contracts are subject to specific restrictions.  

For more information, visit [G-Cloud 14](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM1557.14).
