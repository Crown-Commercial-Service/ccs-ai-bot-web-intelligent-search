Framework Name: Postal Services & Solutions  
Framework Number: RM6280  
Category: Facilities Management  

Objective: This agreement provides postal services to the UK public sector and third sector organisations, offering a broad range of postal goods and services. It aims to help organizations develop their postal services strategy, ensuring they receive the best value for money and facilitating a transition to digital mailroom solutions.  

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: UK public sector, third sector organisations
- Service Types:
  - Mail and parcel collection and delivery services (UK and international)
  - Inbound mail receipt
  - Hybrid mail solutions
  - Security screening
  - Franking machines
  - Mailroom equipment
  - Audits and efficiency reviews
  - Consultancy services

Restrictions:  
- Agreement Duration: 4 years  
- Call Off Contract Periods:  
  - Lot 1: up to 6 years  
  - Lots 2 to 8: up to 7 years  
- Collaboration Partners: YPO and ESPO  
- Replaces: RM6017 Postal Goods, Services and Solutions (expired in November 2023)
