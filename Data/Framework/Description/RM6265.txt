Framework Name: Vehicle Hire Solutions  
Framework Number: RM6265  
Category: Fleet  

Objective:  
Vehicle Hire Solutions RM6265 provides UK public sector organizations with access to a wide range of vehicle hire services. These services support operational fleet needs, business travel, and specialist vehicle requirements both in the UK and overseas. This framework ensures flexible rental durations and access to various vehicle types to meet diverse public sector needs.

Scope:  
- Eligible Sectors: All UK public sector organizations  
- Service Types:  
  - Business travel vehicle hire (e.g., cars for meetings)  
  - Operational fleets (e.g., vans, HGV tippers)  
  - Seasonal needs (e.g., road gritters)  
  - Car share models with hourly rates  
  - Standard short term rentals  
  - Flexible rentals for use over 28 days  
  - Specialist vehicles (e.g., emergency vehicles, healthcare operational vehicles, bespoke vehicles)

Restrictions:  
This agreement will last for 4 years.
