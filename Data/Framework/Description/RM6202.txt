Framework Name: Tail Spend Solution  
Framework Number: RM6202  
Category: Low Value  

Objective: 
Provides public sector organizations with an end-to-end management service for products classified as tail spend, which are typically low-complexity and ad-hoc purchases. It aims to control and make these expenditures visible to avoid significant budget pressures and inefficiencies. 

Scope:
- Eligible Sectors: All public sector organizations
- Service Types: 
  - Digital buying platform with social value criteria filtering 
  - Supply chain management from procurement to payment 
  - Buyer registrations and spend management information services 
  - Permissions and approval settings within hierarchical structures

Restrictions:
- Maximum contract length is 4 years, plus the implementation period where appropriate.  
- Dynamic and pre-vetted supplier base adapts to market demands. 
- Two suppliers available: Unite (Mercateo UK Limited) and Paragon Customer Communications (London) Limited (Office Depot).
