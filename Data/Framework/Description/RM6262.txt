Framework Name: Crown Hosting II  
Framework Number: RM6262  
Category: Cloud and Hosting  

Objective:  
Access secure, reliable, and efficient specialist rooms (data halls) and buildings (data centres) to operate your server, network, and security infrastructure (ICT) from. This data centre co-location service ensures that your IT services work on demand and continuously. Designed primarily for UK public sector organisations, it provides cost-effective and environmentally efficient solutions.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: UK Public Sector Organisations
- Service types:
  - Shared data hall space
  - Private data halls and data centres
  - Physical equipment (e.g., racks)
  - Electricity for ICT operations
  - Optical network connectivity
  - Network cabling within data halls and on-site
  - Setup and operational support
  - ICT relocation services
