**Framework Name**: Construction Works and Associated Services

**Framework Number**: RM6088

**Category**: Construction

**Objective**: This framework delivers a wide range of major and minor building and civil engineering projects for central government and the wider public sector. It provides flexible, innovative contract arrangements to meet varying customer requirements and supports the implementation of government policies and strategies.

**Scope**:
- Eligible Sectors: Central government, wider public sector, and arm’s-length bodies
- Service Types:
  - Major and minor building works
  - Civil engineering projects
  - Housing, high-rise, maritime, airfields 
  - Demolition and decommissioning
  - Construction management

**Restrictions**: 
- The framework includes 11 lots with varying levels of complexity and value (from £0 – £3m up to £80m+).
- Individual lots are divided into regional sub-lots appropriate to the market sector and value range.
- The lower value lots support direct access to SME suppliers.
- Common procurement routes, tendering methods, payment arrangements, and standard forms of contract are available.

For more details, visit: [Construction Works and Associated Services RM6088](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6088)
