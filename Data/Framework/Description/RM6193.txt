Framework Name: Software Design and Implementation Services  
Framework Number: RM6193  
Category: Digital and Technology Services  

Objective: This framework aims to provide specialist, complex, and technical support and implementation services. It assists in deploying new cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems or upgrading legacy IT systems. It caters to entities seeking comprehensive back-office digitization and modernization solutions.

Scope:
- Eligible sectors: Central government departments, wider public sector, third sector organisations
- Service types:
  - Enterprise architecture
  - Business case support
  - Configuration and testing
  - System integration
  - Implementation
  - Data cleansing and migration
  - Change management
  - Training
  - On-boarding
  - Application management support

Restrictions: Maximum call off length of 10 years with flexibility on extension options within this duration. No minimum call-off required. For a clear split between contracts with software providers and professional service suppliers, it is recommended to use RM6194 Back Office Software (BOS) in conjunction.
