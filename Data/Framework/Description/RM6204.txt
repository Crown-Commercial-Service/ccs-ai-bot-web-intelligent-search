**Framework Name:** Rail Legal Services  
**Framework Number:** RM6204  
**Category:** Professional Services  

**Objective:**  
This agreement allows the Department for Transport (DfT) and related organisations to access specialized legal advice and support related to rail services. Developed in partnership with the DfT, it replaces the RM3756 Rail Legal Services agreement, expiring on 31 March 2022. 

**Scope:**  
- **Eligible Sectors:** 
  - Central government departments 
  - Arms-length bodies  
  - Public sector buying organisations  
  - Great British Railways  
- **Service Types:** 
  - Regulatory law  
  - Rail commercial law  
  - Public procurement law  
  - Subsidy law  
  - EU law  
  - International law  
  - Competition law  
  - Dispute resolution and litigation law  
  - Employment law  
  - Environmental law  
  - Health and safety law  
  - Information law including data protection  
  - Information technology law  
  - Insurance law  
  - Intellectual property law  
  - Pensions law  
  - Planning law  
  - Real estate law  
  - Restructuring/insolvency law  
  - Tax law  

**Restrictions:**  
- The wider public sector cannot use this agreement.
- Call-off contracts can be of any value and length but must end no later than 2 years after the agreement end date.
