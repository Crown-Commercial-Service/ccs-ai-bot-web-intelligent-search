Tail Spend Solution: RM6202 
 this greement belongs to Low Value category. 
 Provides public sector organisations with products classified as tail spend. These are typically low-complexity products that organisations buy either on an ad-hoc or regular basis..
 url link: https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6202
