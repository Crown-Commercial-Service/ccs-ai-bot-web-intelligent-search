Public Sector Legal Services: RM6240 
 this greement belongs to Professional Services category. 
 This framework has been established to provide a simple and compliant route to market for a range of legal services requirements primarily to the wider public sector. It covers a wide range of commonly needed areas of law, including contracts, dispute resolution and litigation, contracts and corporate law.
Comprising 3 lots the framework offers both general and more specific legal services..
